HNT Burn-Mint Equilibrium Forecaster

Model: DC burns are USD-fixed (usage × on-chain data price), so HNT burned/day = burn$ ÷ price. Equilibrium price Peq = daily burn $ ÷ daily HNT issuance — the price at which supply is flat. Below Peq the token is net-deflationary; the supply path simulates a market price at k × Peq. Companion to the HNT research report. Model estimates, not investment advice.

How to read it: Peq = burn$/day ÷ (PoC + vault mint + vault sales) per day. It scales linearly with usage and jumps ~4-5x when the vault stops minting (red line = the anchor with vault at zero — the month-37 re-rating embedded in any hold-through thesis). The supply path is reflexive: at k<1 the market price sits below equilibrium, HNT-terms burn exceeds issuance, and supply shrinks — the mechanism that makes the current $0.20 print self-correcting unless vault selling (slider) re-dilutes faster than usage compounds. Yr-1 deflation is the % of today's circulating supply retired net of all issuance at the modeled price.
